Import candidates from CSV

Gives every row of the sheet a paper for its shift. Candidates do not need to have signed in yet: when they sign in with the same email, their paper is waiting.

  1. The sheet needs an email column. It must be the exact Google account the candidate will sign in with.
  2. Add a shift column (1, 2, 3...) to choose shifts yourself, or leave it out and set "number of shifts" below to split the sheet evenly in file order.
  3. Keep a spare shift (e.g. shift 3) out of the import for re-attempts: after the main shifts end, Move Absentees to Shift on the home page sends everyone who never opened their paper (and anyone you Reset) there with that shift's questions.
  4. Optional name and roll columns show up on the results page.
  5. Add the quiz questions first (tagged with their shift). Set each shift's timing before the exam.
  6. Always Preview first. Importing again is safe: papers are replaced, not duplicated, and anyone who already opened their paper is left untouched.
